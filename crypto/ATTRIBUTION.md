## Verification policy

Links are intended to point to primary sources. Because crypto projects change quickly, maintainers should re-check official links, documentation, repository activity, and public-sector sources before merging updates. A working link is not proof that a project is safe, solvent, legal in every jurisdiction, or suitable for investment.
